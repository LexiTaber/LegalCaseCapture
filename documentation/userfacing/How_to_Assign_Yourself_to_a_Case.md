# How to Review Your Cases and Add Yourself to the Case Team

This guide walks through finding a case you're working on in Salesforce and
adding yourself to it, so the case record accurately shows everyone involved.

## Step 1: Open the US Advocacy app

From the App Launcher (the grid icon in the top left), search for and select
**US Advocacy**.

`[SCREENSHOT: App Launcher open, "US Advocacy" highlighted in the search results]`

---

## Step 2: Go to the Legal Cases tab

Once you're in the US Advocacy app, click the **Legal Cases** tab in the
top navigation bar.

`[SCREENSHOT: US Advocacy app navigation bar with the "Legal Cases" tab highlighted]`

---

## Step 3: Find your case

You'll land on a list of Legal Case records. Use the search bar or the column
filters to find the specific case you're working on — searching by case name
or case number is usually fastest.

`[SCREENSHOT: Legal Cases list view with the search bar in use]`

> If you're regularly checking the same set of cases, you can save time by
> creating a personal list view filtered to cases where you're the Lead
> Attorney or Support Staff, or ask your Salesforce admin to set one up for
> the team.

---

## Step 4: Open the case record

Click the case name to open its record page.

`[SCREENSHOT: A single Legal Case record page, fully loaded]`

---

## Step 5: Find the "Case Team" section and click New

Scroll down the case record page until you see the **Case Team** related
list. Click **New**.

`[SCREENSHOT: The Case Team related list on a case record, with the "New" button visible]`

---

## Step 6: Add yourself and your role

In the form that opens:

1. **User** — search for and select your own name.
2. **Staff Role** — choose the role that best describes your work on this
   case: `Attorney`, `Paralegal`, `Legal Assistant/Secretary`, `Law Clerk`,
   or `Of Counsel`.
3. Click **Save**.

`[SCREENSHOT: The "New Case Team Member" form, filled out with a sample name and role]`

You'll now see yourself listed under **Case Team** on the case record.

`[SCREENSHOT: The Case Team related list showing the newly added team member]`

---

## A note on Lead Attorney and Support Staff

Every case also has a single **Lead Attorney** and **Support Staff** field
shown near the top of the case record. These represent the one primary
attorney and one primary support person on the case — they're different from
the Case Team list, which can hold any number of people.

`[SCREENSHOT: The Lead Attorney and Support Staff fields on the case detail page]`

If you're joining a case in one of those two specific roles (not just as a
general team member), let your admin know so they can update those fields —
please don't change them yourself, since only one person can hold each of
those two roles at a time.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't find your case in the list?** Double check the search terms, or ask
  whoever manages the case list whether it's been entered into Salesforce yet.
- **Don't see a "New" button on the Case Team list?** You may need updated
  permissions — contact your Salesforce admin.
- **Added yourself with the wrong role?** Open the Case Team related list,
  click the entry, and edit or delete it.
