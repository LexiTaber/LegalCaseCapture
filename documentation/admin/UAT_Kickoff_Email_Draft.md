## Testing Instructions

As part of your testing, please take a few minutes to navigate the system
and complete some core tasks. Your feedback on what works, what doesn't
work, and what is missing will help us tailor the application to your
needs. You can find an overview of suggested testing below, and a full
how-to guide **[HERE]**.

- **Test 1: Review one of your cases** — Find and open a case you're
  working on, review the details, and flag anything that looks wrong or
  out of date (reply to this email).
- **Test 2: Add yourself to the Case Team** — Do this on 1-2 cases where
  you're not already listed.
- **Test 3: Log a time entry** — On one of those cases, open the Time
  Tracking tab and log an entry (manual hours or start/stop the timer) for
  something you worked on recently.

Before finishing your testing, please share any feedback **[HERE]**.
